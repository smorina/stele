Describe the plate

The defaults favor a trustworthy first build. Open a section below to change the plate, the page grid, the title, the guide band, or the microscope.

Follows the first PDF’s file name until you edit it. Etched into the title band large enough to read without a microscope, unless you set a different title below.
Plate, border and tone size of the glass, unusable edge, clear or dark field, mirroring
Kept clear for handling and edge chips. The title band sits inside this border, not in it.
Pages on glass page size, gutters, magnification ladder
1980 × 2560 µm is the patent's example, about 109:1 for a US Letter page. Each page is scaled proportionally to fit inside this box, never stretched: a page of a different shape is centered with empty space on one side, and “Page on glass” on the right is the size the content actually gets.
0 = pages touch and their own margins separate the text. A 4 µm safety gap is always kept so a page edge can never cross into its neighbour's slot.
Places every page once per size, largest first, so a finder with a loupe is led to the microscope. Costs slots: a 4× copy takes 16.
Feeds the early warning only; the real check is measured after the build.
Title band text, height, band, alignment
Plate sets get “1/3” appended. A title too wide for the space between the corner marks is shrunk to fit and reported.
2.5 mm is readable with the naked eye.
Reserved at the top of the usable area; no pages are placed there. 0 turns the title off.
Vertical budget appears after the plate is calculated.
Guide band description text at the bottom, size, alignment
Up to 6 lines. The page map (plate name, plate k of n, page range, reading order, sources) is always etched above it.
0 = automatic (up to 600 µm, shrunk so every line fits). The text is kept clear of the corner marks and the orientation glyph.
Microscope magnification, NA, wavelength, contrast criterion
Optics estimates appear after the plate is calculated.
These optics do not change the plate. They set the readability simulation's verdict, and after a build you can vary them in the microscope panel without rebuilding.
Each setting shows its default beside its label; a changed setting is tinted and gets a reset button.
Engineering assumptions. The generic writer limits and microscope optics are not vendor facts. They are recorded in every report so they cannot be mistaken for confirmed requirements.
